## November 2025 Update

The November update for tomomai is here! We've focused on two of the most needed features: **Recommendation Filters** and **Recent Play History**. These tools are designed to help you pinpoint the best charts for your rating goals and give you a deeper look into your daily sessions.

## Better Song Recommendations

Finding your next rating push shouldn't be a chore. We've added powerful filters to the **Recommended Songs** page, so you can stop scrolling and start playing.

### What's New?

You can now filter recommendations by:

- **Difficulty**: Toggle between Basic, Advanced, Expert, Master, or Re:MASTER.
- **Level**: Target specific level ranges (e.g., "just show me 14s and 14+").
- **Target Rating**: Find the exact charts you need to reach your next milestone.
- **Version**: Focus on the latest tracks or dive back into specific game versions.

![Recommended Filters](/posts/2025-11-09-recents-and-filters/filters.png)

Whether you're grinding for a specific rating goal or just exploring a certain version, these filters put you in control of your improvement journey.

## Track Your Recent Plays

Ever had a great run and wanted to check the exact breakdown? The new **Recent Songs** feature lets you dive into the details of your latest credits.

### Detailed Stats:
For every play, you can now see:
- **Judge Counts**: Exact Perfect, Great, Good, and Miss counts.
- **Combo & Status**: Max combo and whether you cleared with FC/SSS/etc.
- **DX Score**: A full breakdown of your deluxe score performance.
- **Play Time**: Exactly when you set that new personal best.

![Recent Songs](/posts/2025-11-09-recents-and-filters/recents.png)

### A Quick Tip
To see your recent history for the first time, you'll need to **refetch your data** from the game servers. If you have a long history, this might take a minute—but once it's done, your future plays will sync up much faster!
