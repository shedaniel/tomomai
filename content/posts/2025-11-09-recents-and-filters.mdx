## What's New in November 2025?

We're excited to announce our November update, bringing two highly requested features to tomomai: **Recommendation Filters** and **Recent Songs tracking**. These updates make it easier than ever to find the right charts to improve your rating and review your play history in detail.

## New Feature: Filter Your Recommendations

Finding the perfect song to play next just got a whole lot easier! We've introduced enhanced filtering for your **Recommended Songs** page, giving you precise control over which charts are suggested to you.

### What Can You Filter?

You can now fine-tune your recommended charts by several criteria:

- **Difficulty**: Filter by Basic, Advanced, Expert, Master, or Re:MASTER
- **Level**: Choose specific level ranges (e.g., only show 13+ or 14 charts)
- **Target Rating**: Focus on songs that will help you reach your rating goals
- **Version**: Filter by game version to focus on newer songs or revisit classics
- **And more**: Additional filters to customize your experience

This means you can focus on exactly the kind of charts you want to practice, whether you're grinding specific levels for rating, exploring a particular version's content, or challenging yourself with higher difficulties.

With these filters, you're in complete control of your improvement journey - whether you want to focus on specific levels, difficulties, or versions.

## New Feature: Recent Songs

Ever wished you could review the details of your recent plays? Now you can! The **Recent Songs** feature provides comprehensive information about your play history.

### What Information Can You See?

For each recent credit, you can now view:

- **Notes Details**: Perfect, Great, Good, Miss counts
- **Combo Information**: Your max combo and full combo status
- **DX Score**: Your deluxe score breakdown
- **Timing**: When you played each song
- **And more**: Additional play statistics to help you analyze your performance

### Important Note

To access your recent play history, you may need to **refetch your data** from the game servers. This allows tomomai to retrieve your recent plays for the first time. Please note that this process may take a while, especially if you have a lot of play history. Once the initial fetch is complete, your recent plays will be kept up to date with each subsequent data import.

## How to Use These Features

Both features are available now on your tomomai dashboard:

1. Navigate to the **Recommended Songs** page to start using the new filters
2. Visit the **Recent Songs** section to view your play history
3. If you don't see recent songs, try refetching your data from the settings

## 十一月更新內容

我們很高興宣布十一月的更新，為 tomomai 帶來兩個備受期待的功能：**推薦歌曲篩選器**和**最近遊玩紀錄**。這些更新讓你更容易找到適合提升分數的譜面，並詳細回顧你的遊玩歷史。

## 新功能：推薦歌曲篩選器

尋找下一首要玩的歌曲變得更簡單了！我們為**「推薦歌曲」**頁面引入了增強的篩選功能，讓你能精確控制系統推薦給你的譜面。

### 可以篩選什麼？

你現在可以用多種條件來精確調整推薦的譜面：

- **難度**：按 Basic、Advanced、Expert、Master 或 Re:MASTER 篩選
- **級別**：選擇特定的級別範圍（例如：只顯示 13+ 或 14 的譜面）
- **目標分數**：專注於能幫助你達成分數目標的歌曲
- **版本**：按遊戲版本篩選，專注於新歌或重溫經典
- **更多**：其他自訂選項

這意味著你可以專注於你想練習的譜面類型，無論是為了提升分數而刷特定級別、探索特定版本的內容，還是挑戰更高難度。

有了這些篩選器，你可以完全掌控自己的進步之旅 - 無論你想專注於特定級別、難度或版本。

## 新功能：最近遊玩

想回顧最近遊玩的詳細資訊嗎？現在可以了！**「最近遊玩」**功能提供了你遊玩歷史的完整資訊。

### 可以看到什麼資訊？

對於每次遊玩紀錄，你現在可以查看：

- **音符詳情**：Perfect、Great、Good、Miss 數量
- **連擊資訊**：你的最大連擊數和 Full Combo 狀態
- **DX 分數**：你的 DX 分數明細
- **時間**：你遊玩每首歌的時間
- **更多**：其他遊玩統計資料，幫助你分析表現

### 重要提醒

要查看你的最近遊玩歷史，你可能需要從遊戲伺服器**重新匯入資料**。這讓 tomomai 能首次讀取你的最近遊玩紀錄。請注意，這個過程可能需要一些時間，特別是如果你有大量的遊玩歷史。完成初次匯入後，你的最近遊玩紀錄將在每次後續匯入時保持更新。

## 如何使用這些功能

這兩個功能現在都可以在你的 tomomai 儀表板上使用：

1. 前往**「推薦歌曲」**頁面開始使用新的篩選器
2. 造訪**「最近遊玩」**區域查看你的遊玩歷史
3. 如果看不到最近的歌曲，請嘗試從設定重新匯入資料
