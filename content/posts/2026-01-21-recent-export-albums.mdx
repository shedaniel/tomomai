## Welcome to 2026!

We're excited to kick off 2026 with our January update! This marks the beginning of our **monthly update schedule** - you can now expect regular feature releases and improvements every month. This first update of the year brings two fantastic features: **Recent Credit Export** and **Album Storage**.

## New Feature: Recent Credit Export

Share your performance with style! You can now **export your recent maimai credits as images** directly from tomomai. This feature creates beautiful, detailed visualizations of your play performance that are perfect for sharing with friends or on social media.

### How It Works

Export your recent plays in two convenient ways:

#### 1. Via the tomomai Website
Navigate to your Recent Songs page and export any of your recent credits as an image. The generated image includes:

- **Song Information**: Title, artist, and difficulty
- **Performance Breakdown**: Perfect, Great, Good, Miss counts
- **DX Score**: Your deluxe score for that credit
- **Combo Information**: Max combo and full combo status
- **Achievement Rate**: Your accuracy percentage
- **Rating Impact**: How the play affected your rating

#### 2. Via Discord Bot Commands
Use the Discord bot to quickly share your recent plays:

- `/recent` - Export your recent credit from the International server
- `/recentjp` - Export your recent credit from the Japan server

The bot will generate and send the image directly in Discord, making it easy to share your achievements with your community!

Perfect for celebrating achievements, asking for advice in the community, or tracking your progress over time!

## New Feature: Album Storage

Preserve your memories! We've introduced **optional Album Storage** for your maimai album photos.

### What You Get

- **25MB of Storage**: Enough space for approximately 300 album photos
- **Completely Optional**: You decide whether to use this feature
- **Integrated Experience**: View your album photos directly within tomomai
- **Secure Storage**: Your photos are safely stored and associated with your account

### How Album Storage Works

When you play maimai DX, the game can take album photos during your plays. With Album Storage enabled:

1. Your album photos are automatically saved to your tomomai account when you fetch data
2. Browse your album collection directly on the tomomai website
3. View photos alongside your play history to remember specific sessions
4. Manage your album storage from your settings

Album photos capture special moments from your plays. Previously, these photos would be lost unless you manually saved them from the arcade machine. Now, tomomai can preserve these memories for you automatically.

## 新功能：最近遊玩圖片匯出

用時尚的方式分享你的表現！你現在可以直接從 tomomai **將最近的 maimai 遊玩紀錄匯出為圖片**。此功能會創建精美、詳細的遊玩表現視覺化圖片，非常適合與朋友分享或在社群媒體上發布。

### 運作方式

以兩種便利的方式匯出你的最近遊玩：

#### 1. 透過 tomomai 網站
導航到你的「最近遊玩」頁面，將任何最近的遊玩紀錄匯出為圖片。生成的圖片包含：

- **歌曲資訊**：標題、藝術家和難度
- **表現明細**：Perfect、Great、Good、Miss 數量
- **DX Score**：該次遊玩的 DX 分數
- **連擊資訊**：最大連擊數和 Full Combo 狀態
- **達成率**：你的準確度百分比
- **分數影響**：該次遊玩如何影響你的分數

#### 2. 透過 Discord 機器人指令
使用 Discord 機器人快速分享你的最近遊玩：

- `/recent` - 匯出國際伺服器的最近遊玩紀錄
- `/recentjp` - 匯出日本伺服器的最近遊玩紀錄

機器人會在 Discord 中直接生成並發送圖片，讓你可以輕鬆與社群分享你的成就！

非常適合慶祝成就、在社群中尋求建議或追蹤你的進度！

## 新功能：相簿儲存功能

保存你的回憶！我們推出了 maimai 相簿照片的**可選相簿儲存功能**。

### 你能得到什麼

- **25MB 儲存空間**：足夠存放大約 300 張相簿照片
- **完全可選**：由你決定是否使用此功能
- **整合體驗**：直接在 tomomai 中查看你的相簿照片
- **安全儲存**：你的照片被安全儲存並與你的帳號關聯

### 相簿儲存功能如何運作

當你遊玩 maimai DX 時，遊戲可以在你遊玩期間拍攝相簿照片。啟用相簿儲存後：

1. 當你匯入資料時，你的相簿照片會自動保存到你的 tomomai 帳號
2. 直接在 tomomai 網站上瀏覽你的相簿收藏
3. 查看照片和你的遊玩歷史，回憶特定的遊玩時段
4. 從設定管理你的相簿儲存

相簿照片捕捉特殊時刻 - 你最喜歡的歌曲的完美角度、你第一次通關困難譜面，或只是與朋友的有趣時刻。以前，除非你從街機手動保存，否則這些照片會丟失。現在，tomomai 可以自動為你保存這些回憶。
