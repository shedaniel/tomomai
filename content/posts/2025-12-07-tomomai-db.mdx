## What's New in December 2025?

We're thrilled to announce our biggest update yet! December brings **tomomai DB**, a comprehensive chart browser for exploring all maimai DX songs, along with **customizable theming options** including the highly requested dark mode. These features transform how you discover and interact with charts on tomomai.

## New Feature: tomomai DB

**tomomai DB** is your new home for exploring the entire maimai DX song library. This powerful chart browser gives you unprecedented control over how you view, search, and organize charts.

### What Can You Do?

The tomomai DB page offers comprehensive tools for browsing charts:

- **Advanced Sorting**: Arrange charts by level, difficulty, or even alphabetically
- **Flexible Filtering**: Filter by level, difficulty, chart designer, genre, and game version
- **Smart Grouping**: Group charts to organize your view exactly how you want
- **Powerful Search**: Search across songs, artists, and genres to quickly find what you're looking for
- **Multiple View Modes**: Switch between grid and list views for your preferred browsing experience

### Detailed Song Information

Clicking on any song reveals comprehensive information about that chart:

- **Technical Details**: Note count, BPM, chart designer, and more
- **Difficulty Breakdown**: See all available difficulties and their levels at a glance
- **Server Availability**: Check which servers (International/Japan) have the chart
- **Chart Statistics**: Detailed note type breakdowns (Tap, Hold, Slide, Touch, Break)
- **YouTube Integration**: Direct link to search the song on YouTube for preview or gameplay videos

Now everything is in one place - whether you're hunting for specific charts by designer, targeting charts at your level, or just browsing for inspiration.

Visit tomomai DB: [https://tomomai.lol/db/songs](https://tomomai.lol/db/songs)

## New Feature: Customizable Themes

Your tomomai experience just got more personal! We've added comprehensive theming options that let you customize the look and feel of the entire website.

### Choose Your Colors

Select from multiple color themes to match your personal preference. Each theme has been carefully designed to maintain readability and visual appeal while giving you the customization you want.

### Dark Mode Is Here!

The most requested feature has arrived: **Dark Mode**! For the first time, you can use tomomai with a sleek dark theme that's easier on the eyes during late-night grinding sessions. Dark mode isn't just an inverted color scheme—it's been thoughtfully designed to provide optimal contrast and readability in low-light conditions.

Access theme settings from the navigation bar to switch between themes anytime!

## How to Use These Features

Both features are available now:

1. **Explore tomomai DB**: Navigate to the DB section from the main menu or visit [tomomai.lol/db/songs](https://tomomai.lol/db/songs)
2. **Change Your Theme**: Click the "Theme" button in the navigation bar to choose your preferred color scheme and enable dark mode
3. **Customize Your View**: Use the filtering and grouping options in tomomai DB to create your perfect browsing experience

## 十二月更新內容

我們很高興宣布目前為止最大的更新！十二月帶來了 **tomomai DB**，一個可以探索所有 maimai DX 歌曲的綜合譜面瀏覽器，以及**自訂主題選項**，包括眾所期待的深色模式。這些功能改變了你在 tomomai 上發現和互動譜面的方式。

## 新功能：tomomai DB

**tomomai DB** 是你探索整個 maimai DX 歌曲庫的新家。這個強大的譜面瀏覽器讓你能前所未有地控制如何查看、搜尋和組織譜面。

### 可以做什麼？

tomomai DB 頁面提供了全面的譜面瀏覽工具：

- **進階排序**：按級別、難度或字母順序排列譜面
- **靈活篩選**：按級別、難度、譜師、分類和遊戲版本篩選
- **智慧分組**：將譜面分組，完全按照你想要的方式組織
- **強大搜尋**：跨歌曲、藝術家和分類搜尋，快速找到你要的內容
- **多種檢視模式**：在網格和列表檢視之間切換，選擇你偏好的瀏覽方式

### 詳細歌曲資訊

點擊任何歌曲可顯示該譜面的完整資訊：

- **技術細節**：Note 數、BPM、譜師等
- **難度明細**：一目了然地查看所有可用難度及其級別
- **伺服器可用性**：檢查哪些伺服器（國際版/日版）有該譜面
- **譜面統計**：詳細的 Note 類型明細（Tap、Hold、Slide、Touch、Break）
- **YouTube 整合**：直接連結到 YouTube 搜尋該歌曲的預覽或遊玩影片

現在一切都在一個地方 - 無論你是在尋找特定譜師的譜面、攻略你級別的譜面，還是只是瀏覽尋找靈感。

造訪 tomomai DB：[https://tomomai.lol/db/songs](https://tomomai.lol/db/songs)

## 新功能：自訂主題

你的 tomomai 體驗變得更加個人化了！我們新增了全面的主題選項，讓你可以自訂整個網站的外觀和風格。

### 選擇你的顏色

從多種顏色主題中選擇，以符合你的個人偏好。每個主題都經過精心設計，在提供你想要的自訂功能的同時，保持可讀性和視覺吸引力。

### 深色模式來了！

最受期待的功能已經到來：**深色模式**！你現在可以第一次使用時尚的深色主題來使用 tomomai，在深夜刷分時更加護眼。深色模式不只是反轉的配色方案——它經過精心設計，在低光環境中提供最佳對比度和可讀性。

從導覽列存取主題設定，隨時切換主題！

## 如何使用這些功能

這兩個功能現在都已上線：

1. **探索 tomomai DB**：從主選單導航到 DB 區域，或造訪 [tomomai.lol/db/songs](https://tomomai.lol/db/songs)
2. **更改主題**：點擊導覽列中的「Theme」按鈕，選擇你偏好的配色方案並啟用深色模式
3. **自訂檢視**：使用 tomomai DB 中的篩選和分組選項，打造你的完美瀏覽體驗
