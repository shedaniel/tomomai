## December 2025: Our Biggest Update Yet

We’ve been busy! This month, we're dropping **tomomai DB**, a powerful way to explore the maimai DX song library, plus **Custom Themes** including the most requested feature ever: Dark Mode. 

## New Feature: tomomai DB

**tomomai DB** is your new one-stop shop for everything maimai DX. Whether you're looking for a specific chart or just browsing for inspiration, we've got you covered.

### What can you do?
- **Advanced Sorting**: Sort by level, difficulty, or alphabetically.
- **Granular Filters**: Filter by level, difficulty, chart designer (譜師), genre, or game version.
- **Flexible Views**: Switch between grid and list modes to find what suits you best.
- **Deep Search**: Quickly find songs by title, artist, or genre.

### Detailed Chart Info
Clicking a song now gives you the full breakdown:
- **Technical Stats**: Total notes, BPM, and chart designer info.
- **Detailed Note Distribution**: Breakdown of Tap, Hold, Slide, Touch, and Break counts.
- **Server Tracking**: See if the song is available on International or JP servers.
- **Quick Links**: Direct YouTube search links to find gameplay videos instantly.

Check it out here: [https://tomomai.lol/db/songs](https://tomomai.lol/db/songs)

## New Feature: Customizable Themes

You can finally make tomomai look exactly how you want. We've added a theme engine that lets you change the site's look and feel.

### Dark Mode is here!
No more blinding white screens during late-night sessions! Our new **Dark Mode** is carefully designed for high readability in low-light conditions. 

### How to customize:
Just click the **"Theme"** button in the navigation bar to toggle Dark Mode or pick from our range of color presets.

## 12 月更新內容

這次更新內容我們帶來了功能強大的 **tomomai DB**（譜面資料庫），以及大家敲碗已久的**自訂主題**——沒錯，深色模式終於來了！

## 新功能：tomomai DB 查歌神器

**tomomai DB** 是你探索 maimai DX 歌單的強力工具。不論是想研究特定譜面，還是單純想找歌打，這裡應有盡有。

### 重點功能：
- **進階排序**：可按等級、難度或字母順序排列。
- **精準篩選**：支援等級、難度、譜師、樂曲類別及遊戲版本篩選。
- **自定義檢視**：提供網格與列表兩種模式，看哪種最順手。
- **強大搜尋**：直接搜尋歌名、繪師或類別。

### 詳細資料一覽：
點進任何歌曲，你就能看到：
- **技術數據**：總物量、BPM、譜師是誰。
- **物量分配**：Tap、Hold、Slide、Touch、Break 的詳細數量。
- **伺服器資訊**：確認該曲目目前在國際版或日版是否有實裝。
- **YouTube 連結**：一鍵搜尋 YouTube 上的譜面或手元影片。

傳送門：[https://tomomai.lol/db/songs](https://tomomai.lol/db/songs)

## 新功能：自訂主題介面

現在你可以把 tomomai 變成自己喜歡的樣子了！我們加入了全新的主題切換功能。

### 深色模式正式登場！
熬夜刷分黨的救星來了！我們特別設計了**深色模式**，不只是單純把顏色反轉，而是針對低光源環境調整了對比度，讓你在半夜看螢幕不再刺眼。

### 如何設定：
點擊導覽列上的 **「Theme」** 按鈕，就可以自由切換深色/淺色模式，或選擇我們預設的其他配色方案。
