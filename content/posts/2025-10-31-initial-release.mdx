## What is tomomai?

I’ve been working on a new tool to help players track their progress. tomomai (ともマイ) is a modern web application for tracking and analyzing your **maimai DX** scores.

## Why did I create tomomai?

tomomai was a special creation of mine that started with me using other maimai tools like mimi dx bot and maishift. They both were great and awesome tools, but I thought I could do better. I wanted to create a tool that was more user-friendly, more customizable, and more powerful than anything else out there.

I utilized my experience in web and full-stack development to create tomomai, a website that supports different locales and provides great UI/UX.

Help me improve tomomai for your language! [https://crowdin.com/project/tomomai](https://crowdin.com/project/tomomai)

## Features

- Import your scores directly and keep a history of your progress over time.
- Browse and search all maimai DX songs with detailed information.
- View detailed stats about your play history and performance.
- Support for International and Japanese servers, with other regions planned?
- Share your profile with friends and the community.
- Fully open sourced! Contributions are welcome! :DD

## Getting Started

1. Sign in with your Discord account
2. Set up your token to fetch your scores
3. Start tracking your progress!

We also have a growing discord community!
[https://discord.gg/jZqQHr3UDq](https://discord.gg/jZqQHr3UDq)

We hope you enjoy using tomomai. Stay tuned for more updates!

### Extras: How the name came about

The project was initially called maimai-friends, the idea was that you could share your profile or your scores with friends, and honestly I liked it a lot.

Well, it was until I needed to find a domain name for the project website. All of them suck don't they?

- maifriends.net
- maifriends.moe
- maifriends.cat
- maimaifriends.com
- maimaifriends.net

My friend Vanadium_Ice suggested tomomai.net for the project name. The conversation was just:

```
> me: all seems bad idk
> Vanadium_Ice: how about tomomai.net (I basically changed the name to Japanese)
> me: ~~いっしょにまい~~
> Vanadium_Ice: 一緒にまいまいするマイ
> Vanadium_Ice: 🗿
> me: 🗿
```

<br/>
a--nd that was it.

tomomai ともマイ is a great name. It basically has these components:
- <ruby>友<rt>とも</rt></ruby> means friends
- <ruby>共<rt>とも</rt></ruby>に means doing something together
- and it is only one syllable away from <ruby>伴い<rt>ともない</rt></ruby>
- the idea to style it as ともマイ was that ともまい looks too similar to ともない

## tomomai 是什麼？

最近我寫了一個幫大家紀錄 **maimai DX** 成績的小工具，叫作 **tomomai (ともマイ)**。它是一個現代化的網頁程式，專門用來追蹤跟分析你的個人分數。

## 為什麼要做 tomomai？

tomomai 的誕生，是因為我以前常用 mimi dx bot 或 maishift。雖然這類工具都很棒，但我總覺得可以再進一步——例如讓介面更友善、提供更多自定義的選擇，還有更強大的分析功能。

我利用自己在網頁與 Full-stack 開發的經驗製作了 tomomai，除了支援多國語言，也特別注重 UI/UX 的操作體驗。

如果你想幫忙改善繁體中文以及其他語言的翻譯，歡迎到這裡：[https://crowdin.com/project/tomomai](https://crowdin.com/project/tomomai)

## 功能亮點

- **直接匯入成績**：輕鬆紀錄你的進度與分數歷史。
- **樂曲資料庫**：瀏覽並搜尋所有 maimai DX 樂曲的詳細資訊。
- **詳細數據統計**：分析你的遊玩紀錄與表現。
- **支援多地區伺服器**：目前支援國際版 (International) 與日版 (Japanese)，之後考慮加入更多區域？
- **分享個人 Profile**：跟朋友或社群推廣你的戰績。
- **完全開源**：專案程式碼通通公開，歡迎大佬們一起來貢獻！ :DD

### 開發後話：名字是怎麼來的？

這個專案原本打算叫 `maimai-friends`。初衷是想讓大家能跟朋友分享 Profile 或成績，老實說，我本來還挺滿意這個名字的。

直到我準備買 Domain（網域名稱）時，發現好記的都被搶光了，剩下的都超難看：

- maifriends.net
- maifriends.moe
- maifriends.cat
- maimaifriends.com
- maimaifriends.net

後來朋友 Vanadium_Ice 隨口提了一句：「不如叫 `tomomai.net` 吧？」
當時的對話大概是這樣：

```text
> 我：那些網域都很爛，沒靈感
> Vanadium_Ice：tomomai.net 怎樣？（我直接把它日文化了）
> 我：~~いっしょにまい~~ (一起 maimai)
> Vanadium_Ice：一緒にまいまいするマイ (一起 maimai 吧 mai)
> Vanadium_Ice：🗿
> 我：🗿
```

<br/>
於是就這麼定案了。

**tomomai (ともマイ)** 其實有很多層意思：
- <ruby>友<rt>とも</rt></ruby> 代表朋友
- <ruby>共<rt>とも</rt></ruby>に 代表「一起做某事」
- 唸起來跟 <ruby>伴い<rt>ともない</rt></ruby> (伴隨) 只差一個音節
- 刻意寫成 **ともマイ** 是為了不讓它看起來太像一般的日文字「ともない」

希望這個名字能傳達出「和朋友一起打機」的感覺！
