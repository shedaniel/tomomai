## What is tomomai?

I’ve been working on a new tool to help players track their progress. tomomai (ともマイ) is a modern web application for tracking and analyzing your **maimai DX** scores.

## Why did I create tomomai?

tomomai was a special creation of mine that started with me using other maimai tools like mimi dx bot and maishift. They both were great and awesome tools, but I thought I could do better. I wanted to create a tool that was more user-friendly, more customizable, and more powerful than anything else out there.

I utilized my experience in web and full-stack development to create tomomai, a website that supports different locales and provides great UI/UX.

Help me improve tomomai for your language! [https://crowdin.com/project/tomomai](https://crowdin.com/project/tomomai)

## Features

- Import your scores directly and keep a history of your progress over time.
- Browse and search all maimai DX songs with detailed information.
- View detailed stats about your play history and performance.
- Support for International and Japanese servers, with other regions planned?
- Share your profile with friends and the community.
- Fully open sourced! Contributions are welcome! :DD

## Getting Started

1. Sign in with your Discord account
2. Set up your token to fetch your scores
3. Start tracking your progress!

We also have a growing discord community!
[https://discord.gg/jZqQHr3UDq](https://discord.gg/jZqQHr3UDq)

We hope you enjoy using tomomai. Stay tuned for more updates!
