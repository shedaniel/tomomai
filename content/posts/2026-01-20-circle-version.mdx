## maimai DX CiRCLE Launches January 22, 2026

Get ready! **maimai DX CiRCLE** is releasing for the International region on **January 22, 2026**.

## Important: Preserve Your Data Before the Update

While tomomai fully supports data conversion between different maimai versions, **we strongly recommend that you fetch your data one final time right before the version cutoff**.

When maimai DX updates to a new version, certain data from the previous version may become unavailable. By fetching your data before the version update, you ensure that your complete play history is preserved in tomomai's database.

### How to Preserve Your Data

1. Fetch your data as close as possible to the version cutoff time (January 22, 2026)
2. Make sure your token is properly configured in tomomai
3. Go to your settings and manually trigger a data fetch
4. Wait for the process to complete fully
5. Verify that your recent plays and scores are showing correctly

Once maimai DX CiRCLE launches, you can continue using tomomai as normal. Your previous data will remain safely stored, and tomomai will handle version conversion automatically.
