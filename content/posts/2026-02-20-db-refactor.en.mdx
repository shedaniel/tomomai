## February Update: Visualizing Your Progress

We’re keeping our **monthly update commitment** going strong! This February, we’re moving beyond just tracking scores and moving into tracking your *achievements*. We've overhauled the Stats page and added a highly requested feature for plate hunters.

## New Feature: Player Stats & Plates Progress

If you're chasing those elusive **Plates** like 「極」, 「將」, or 「舞」, this update is for you.

### Revamped Stats Page
We’ve redesigned the **Statistics** page to give you a clearer bird's-eye view of your achievement distribution. You can now see a full breakdown of your grades (from D up to SSS+) for any specific version or difficulty.

![Stats Page](/posts/2026-02-20-db-refactor/stats.png)

### Track Your Plate Progress
Click the new **"View Plates Progress"** button to see exactly how close you are to your next plate.
- **Visual Progress**: See your completion count for categories like "All FC or above" (極).
- **Missing Chart Finder**: tomomai will now list the specific songs you are missing to complete a plate requirement. No more manual checking against a spreadsheet!

![Stats Page](/posts/2026-02-20-db-refactor/plates.png)

## New Feature: Dedicated Changelogs

You're looking at it! We've introduced a proper **Changelog & News** section at `/db/posts`. From now on, all major updates, version notes, and community announcements will be archived here so you can keep track of what's changing in tomomai.

## New Feature: Album Management
Last month we introduced Album Storage, and this month we’re giving you more control over it. You can now **delete specific images** from your tomomai album. This makes it easier to stay within the 25MB limit by clearing out accidental shots or blurry photos while keeping your best memories.

![Delete Album](/posts/2026-02-20-db-refactor/delete.png)

## Privacy & Terms of Service
As tomomai grows, we want to ensure transparency regarding your data. Starting today, all users will be asked to agree to our **Terms of Service** and **Privacy Policy**.

**The TL;DR (Not Legal Advice):**
- **You own your data**; it is never sold to third parties.
- Data is encrypted and stored securely on servers in **Japan**.
- Processing is done in accordance with **Hong Kong SAR laws**.
- tomomai is **Open Source (AGPL-3.0)** and independent from SEGA.

## Backend: Data Accuracy Refactor
We’ve completely rebuilt our song data pipeline to be more accurate.
- **Better Version Handling**: Songs like *Supersonic Generation* are now correctly tagged for their specific release versions in both Japan and International regions.
- **Duplicate Handling**: We can now properly distinguish between different songs that have identical names (e.g., *Link*).
- **Precise Constants**: We've updated internal levels for **65 charts** (including *Dazzle hop* and *ニルヴの心臓* Master charts) using a new double-fallback system for maximum accuracy.
