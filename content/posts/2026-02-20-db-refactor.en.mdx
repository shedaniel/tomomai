## The Monthly Beat Goes On

Welcome to the February update! As promised, we are sticking to our **monthly update commitment** to ensure tomomai keeps evolving. This month is a mix of "keeping things official" with new site policies and a massive "under the hood" refactor to make our song data more reliable than ever.

## New Feature: Dedicated Changelogs

You're looking at it! We've introduced a proper **Changelog & News** section at `/db/posts`. From now on, all major updates, version notes, and community announcements will be archived here so you can keep track of what's changing in tomomai.

## Privacy & Terms of Service

As tomomai grows, we want to make sure your data is handled responsibly. Starting today, new users will be asked to agree to our **Terms of Service** and **Privacy Policy** during registration.

**The TL;DR (Not Legal Advice):**
- **You own your data**; we will never sell it to third parties.
- Your data is encrypted and stored securely on servers in **Japan**.
- Your data is processed in accordance with **Hong Kong SAR laws**.
- tomomai is **Open Source (AGPL-3.0)**.
- We are an independent tool and **not affiliated with SEGA**.

## Backend Overhaul: Better Data, Fewer Errors

We spent a lot of time this month refactoring how tomomai handles song information. We moved away from a single, giant update script to a much smarter "service-based" pipeline.

**What does this mean for you?**
- **No more "Link" confusion**: We can now properly distinguish between different songs that share the same name (like the two different versions of *Link*).
- **UTAGE (宴) Support**: Better internal management for UTAGE charts (full support is still cooking!).
- **Region-Specific Accuracy**: Some songs are added in different versions depending on the region (e.g., *Supersonic Generation* is PRiSM PLUS in JP but PRiSM in Intl). tomomai now tracks this correctly for both.
- **Improved Level Data**: We now use a "double fallback" system (pulling from dxrating and otoge-db) to ensure International level data and precise constants are as accurate as possible.

## Level Adjustments (65 Changes)

Based on the new data pipeline, we have updated the precise internal levels (constants) for 65 charts. Notable adjustments include:
- **Dazzle hop (DX)**: Master precise level increased from 14.0 to 14.2.
- **ニルヴの心臓 (DX)**: Master precise level increased from 14.0 to 14.3.
- *...and 62 other minor tweaks to Advanced, Expert, and Master charts.*
